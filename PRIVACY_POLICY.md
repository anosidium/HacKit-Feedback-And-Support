# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how HacKit handles information.

HacKit is developed and maintained by an independent developer. The app is designed with privacy in mind and does not collect personal data beyond what is strictly necessary for it to function correctly and to support ongoing development.

HacKit does not track you across other apps or websites, does not profile you and does not share any information for marketing or advertising purposes.

---

### Information You Provide

#### Hacker News Usage

You are not required to create an account to use HacKit. The app can browse content from Hacker News without authentication.

#### Local Data

Cached stories, folders and preferences are stored locally on your device.

This data is not transmitted to the developer.

---

### Analytics

HacKit uses [TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com) to collect anonymised, privacy-preserving analytics. TelemetryDeck gathers high-level signals that help assess app performance and general usage patterns, such as:

- feature usage
- app version
- anonymised device environment information such as system appearance, language and macOS version

TelemetryDeck does not collect personally identifiable information, does not track you across other apps or services and does not create user profiles.

Further details are available in the TelemetryDeck's [privacy policy](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/).

---

### Crash Reporting

HacKit uses [Sentry](https://sentry.io/) to collect crash reports. These reports include technical information about the state of the app at the time of a crash (such as stack traces and OS version) in order to diagnose and resolve faults.

Further details are available in the Sentry’s [privacy policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/).

---

### Network Communication

HacKit retrieves content from Hacker News over secure HTTPS connections directly between your device and the Hacker News infrastructure.

No information is routed through servers owned or controlled by the developer.

---

### Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect improvements to the app or clarifications in wording.

The latest version will always be available in the repository from which this document is accessed.
